# Interview questions

A reusable bank. Copy the relevant questions into the offer folder before an
interview and add the answers you gave.

## About the role

- Why is this role open now?
- What does success look like after 3, 6 and 12 months?
- Which decisions does this role own, and which are shared?
- What is the biggest technical risk the team faces this year?

## About the platform

- How many clusters, cloud accounts and environments do you run?
- Who owns the platform: a central team or product teams?
- What is the deployment process from commit to production?
- How do you handle secrets, access and audit trails?
- What does your Terraform module structure look like?
- How do you test infrastructure changes?

## Reliability and operations

- What is the on-call rotation, and how often does it page?
- What are your SLOs, and who reviews them?
- What was the last incident, and what changed afterwards?
- How do you measure cost per team or per service?

## Team and culture

- How does the team make decisions when there is disagreement?
- What does a typical week look like?
- How is feedback given and received?
- What happened to the last person in this role?

## Compensation and contract

- What is the salary range for this role?
- Is it a permanent employment contract or B2B?
- How often are salaries reviewed?
- What benefits are included?
- Is the role fully remote, and are there any office expectations?

## Closing

- Is there anything in my background you would like me to clarify?
- What are the next steps and the expected timeline?
